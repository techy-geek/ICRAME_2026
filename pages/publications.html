Publication Guidelines
  1. All accepted and presented papers at ICRAME 2026 will be published in the Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering (LNME) Series conference proceedings, subject to publisher approval and compliance with publication standards.
  2. Selected high-quality papers, based on the recommendations of the Technical Program Committee, will be considered for publication in special issues of reputed SCI, SCOPUS, SCIE , and ESCI indexed journals. The list of partnering journals will be announced shortly.
